Service Desert Index

Zip Code Service Desert Index Desert Tier Total Recommendations Avg Facilities per Search Follow-Through Rate Total Care Plans
How the Service Desert Index is calculated:
• 40% weight: Therapy demand intensity (frequency and severity of recommendations)
• 35% weight: Facility supply gap (how few recommended facilities were found per search)
• 25% weight: Follow-through gap (how rarely families act on referrals in this area)
A score of 1.0 = maximum desert conditions. A score of 0.0 = fully served community.
